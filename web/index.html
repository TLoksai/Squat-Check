Check your squat against the standard

Upload a side-view video of your barbell back squat. We track your hips, knees, ankles and the bar, find each rep, and check it against the reference squat standard, with the page number for every rule.

or

Your video is processed on this server to produce the analysis and is not shared. You can delete it from the results page.

How to record

  • Side view. Camera square to your side, not in front or behind.
  • Hip height, level, still. Use a tripod or lean the phone on something.
  • Whole body and the plate in the picture, feet included.
  • 1 to 10 reps, good light, plain background if you can.
